# Indoor And Transfer Data

The OSS-safe schema-v2 baseline bundles no indoor diagrams, transfer paths, or static station
map files. `verifiedTransferContexts.covered` is therefore zero for every catalog city.

Official layout references are HTTPS landing pages only. They do not imply that a diagram was
copied, redistributed, verified on site, or converted into navigable geometry. Any future
indoor-data contribution needs a separately reviewed source, redistribution right, checksum,
and verification status before it can affect coverage.

The retired Beijing diagram tracer, its 41 generated records, and all legacy pack importers were
removed. The Swift indoor-navigation interfaces remain dormant so a future on-site-verified pilot
can be added without fabricating geometry in this release.
